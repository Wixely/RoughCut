## Fixtures and concrete outcomes

Reproducible argument arrays and expectations live in `tests/RoughCut.Tests/ExportTests.cs`. All inputs are synthetic. Generated media, source/project/output hashes and export reports live under ignored `artifacts/tests/`; no personal media was used.

The base Matroska fixture is four seconds of 160x96 video at 10 fps, encoded as RGB24 PNG frames. Six binary bars visually encode each frame index. Audio is mono 48 kHz signed 16-bit PCM from `aevalsrc=0.2*sin(2*PI*(200*t+30*t*t)):s=48000:d=4`, packetized into 4,800-sample blocks. Supplied SRT includes one cue crossing a removed boundary, one entirely removed cue and one cue in the later retained slice.

Edits retain `[2s,3s)` followed by `[0s,1s)`. The strict-copy result has 20 frames, 96,000 PCM samples, one join and zero observed audio timestamp error. Its video and audio packet payloads match the selected source packets. Every decoded frame and audio sample matches the requested mapping. An independent RGB pixel reader decodes the visible IDs and confirms frames 20–29 followed by 0–9. Two retimed cues remain; the removed cue is absent. The export report's output SHA-256 matches the generated file and the source hash remains unchanged.

The crop variant uses rectangle `(8,4,80,48)` on both retained slices. Strict-copy preflight rejects it. Without `--allow-encode`, export produces no final directory. With encoding permitted, video is FFV1 at 80x48 and audio stays copied; decoded cropped-frame hashes and all PCM samples match.

The H.264 variant has a 40-frame GOP, three configured B-frames and PCM audio. Strict copy is rejected, even though frame flags exist. Encoding to FFV1 while copying the audio passes the same full decoded-content and timestamp checks. A separate PCM variant uses 1,024-sample packets: strict mode rejects its incompatible cuts/rounded packet timing; prefer-copy encodes sample-trimmed PCM while keeping PNG video copied, with exact source-sample equality.

Cancellation after staging begins and a deliberately incorrect export executable both leave no final output or staging directory, and preserve the input hash. These are recorded execution checks, not assumptions about FFmpeg exit behavior.

## Failures found and corrected

- A font-based `drawtext` fixture crashed the installed FFmpeg build. The committed fixture uses font-free binary frame bars and adds no font dependency.
- H.264 packets can omit initial DTS values in Matroska. Probe parsing now preserves missing DTS instead of assuming it exists; this never grants copy eligibility. PCM and PNG copy still require exact timestamps.
- Input-side seeking during PCM stream copy inherited video-keyframe preroll, causing invalid timestamps when slices were concatenated. Copy selection now scans from the beginning with output-side start/duration selection. The H.264/audio-copy regression passes after this correction.
